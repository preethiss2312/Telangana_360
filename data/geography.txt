Telangana is located on the Deccan Plateau in southern India. It covers about 112,077 square kilometres and had a population of about 3.5 crore in the 2011 census. Hyderabad is its capital and largest city.

Telangana is bordered by Maharashtra to the north and northwest, Chhattisgarh to the northeast, Karnataka to the west, and Andhra Pradesh to the south and east. It has no coastline, so it is a landlocked state.

Telangana has 33 districts. The new districts Mulugu and Narayanpet were added in 2019 after earlier reorganisation of the old ten districts. Important cities include Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, Khammam and Mahabubnagar.

The Godavari and the Krishna are the two major rivers of Telangana. The Godavari flows through the north and east of the state, and the Krishna flows through the south. Other important rivers include the Manjira, Musi, Bhima, Penganga, Pranahita and Dindi.

Major irrigation projects include Nagarjuna Sagar and Srisailam on the Krishna river, Sriram Sagar (Pochampad) on the Godavari, and Nizam Sagar on the Manjira. The Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Project on the Godavari was inaugurated in June 2019 and lifts water to dry regions of the state.

The land of Telangana is mostly a plateau with hills, rocky outcrops and the Nallamala hills in the south. Red soils and black cotton soils are common. Rice, cotton, maize, chilli and turmeric are important crops.

The climate is tropical and semi-arid. Summers are very hot, with temperatures often above 40 degrees Celsius in April and May. The southwest monsoon brings most of the rain between June and September.

Telangana has forests in Adilabad, Khammam, Mulugu and Nagarkurnool districts. The Kawal Tiger Reserve and the Amrabad Tiger Reserve are protected areas for tigers, and Eturnagaram Wildlife Sanctuary is another important forest area.

Telangana has two official languages, Telugu and Urdu. Hyderabad is also a major centre for information technology, with HITEC City, and for pharmaceuticals and biotechnology, with Genome Valley. T-Hub, a startup incubator, began in Hyderabad in 2015.
